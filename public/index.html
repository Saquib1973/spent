Everything you need, nothing you don’t

All your accounts

Bank, savings, credit cards, cash and wallets, each with its own balance, plus what you owe on cards.

Works offline

Add and delete with no signal. Everything is kept on your phone and syncs in one go when you’re back online.

Your categories

Start with sensible defaults, then add, rename, recolour or delete them to fit how you spend.

Clear insights

Spending trend, categories, weekday patterns and income versus spending over six months.

Safe to spend

Your budget turned into one number: how much you can spend per day for the rest of the month.

Receipts
